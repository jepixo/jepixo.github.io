About

The Management Department of Christ College Pune seeks to equip graduates with a solid foundation in management theory and practice in order to help them succeed across disciplines. Graduates of the BBA, BBA (International Business) and BBA (Computer Application) programmes are well-prepared to succeed in a fast-paced, industry-aligned career.

Message from the HOD

A hearty welcome to the Department of Management, Christ College Pune, which provides a distinctive environment for imparting business and technical studies across Business Administration, International Business, and Computer Applications. We provide practical, experiential, participative and intellectual learning experiences through internships, field visits, corporate talks, business tech fests and seminars, cultivating confidence, commitment and responsibility in our students.

Ms. Deepa Sujith
