About

The Department of Science at Christ College, established in 2007, is committed to delivering high-quality education in the field of science. The department offers undergraduate (B.Sc. in Computer Science) and postgraduate (M.Sc. in Computer Science) programmes designed to equip students with a strong academic foundation and practical skills, supported by dedicated faculty and lab-driven, project-based learning.

Message from the HOD

Welcome to the Department of Science at Christ College, Pune. In 2007, we established our department with the vision of producing academically, morally and ethically sound IT professionals. Our department has consistently focused on equipping students with the essential skills required to thrive in the fast-evolving landscape of IT and research. The MSc in Computer Science was introduced to bridge the gap between academic knowledge and industry needs.

Mrs. Nilima Shingate
